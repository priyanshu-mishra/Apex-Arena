Font supplements in this folder
================================

archivo-symbols.woff2 and martian-symbols.woff2 are small same-font subsets
(arrows and math symbols) of the Archivo and Martian Mono typefaces. Both are
licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1.
